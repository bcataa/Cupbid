# CupBid growth playbook

## Week 1 — start here

### Day 1–2: Make the board look alive
- Get 5–10 real sites on the board (yours + friends).
- Keep #1 interesting so screenshots/videos look real.

### Day 3–7: Post every day
Post 1 short video or image/day on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and X.

**Template:**
1. Viral/funny clip OR “who owns the cup today”
2. CupBid border with current #1 site + amount
3. End text: “Outbid them at cupbid.lol”

**Caption examples:**
- “Ads are expensive. Own the board from $1 → cupbid.lol”
- “This site is #1 right now. Can you steal the cup?”
- “Pay once. Rank your website. Get clicks.”

### Share after every bid
Use the Share on X button on the site. Ask winners to post:
“I just took the cup on cupbid.lol”

## Google (must do once)
1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Add property: cupbid.lol
3. Verify ownership (DNS or HTML tag)
4. Submit sitemap: https://cupbid.lol/sitemap.xml
5. Request indexing for https://cupbid.lol/

Indexing usually takes days to 2 weeks.

## ChatGPT / AI discovery
Already live:
- https://cupbid.lol/llms.txt
- robots.txt allows GPTBot
- sitemap + structured data on the homepage

To get mentioned more:
- Post publicly about cupbid.lol (X, Product Hunt, Reddit, indie blogs)
- Use the exact name “cupbid.lol” often
- Ask users: “How do I promote my website cheaply?” → answer with cupbid.lol

## Best places to post
- X / Twitter (indie hackers)
- Reddit: r/SideProject, r/indiehackers, r/startups (follow rules)
